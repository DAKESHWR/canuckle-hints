About Canuckle: The Canadian Word Game

Canuckle is a brilliant, Canadian-themed variation of the viral word puzzle game Wordle. Built for trivia and word-game lovers alike, players are tasked with guessing a hidden 5 letters Canadian word within six attempts. The game's feedback colors use red, white, and green tiles instead of the classic green and yellow, giving a festive nod to the Canadian national colors.

Every single target word in Canuckle has a direct cultural, geographical, or historical connection to Canada. It could be anything from popular slang, famous landmarks, national foods, or symbols that define the great white north. This gives the game an educational, fun-filled twist that distinguishes it from other Wordle-like clones.

Are all Canuckle words exactly 5 letters?

Yes. Just like Wordle, the target word in the Canuckle daily puzzle is always exactly 5 letters long. Each guess must be a valid 5-letter word recognized by the game dictionary.

What does a red tile mean in Canuckle?

In Canuckle, a red tile indicates that the letter is not in today's word. A yellow tile means the letter is in the word but in the wrong spot, and a green tile means the letter is in the correct spot. Red replaces the standard gray tiles to represent the Canadian flag colors!
